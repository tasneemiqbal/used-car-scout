# Judge access and testing

## Access
App: https://usedcarscout.lovable.app
Start on the homepage and choose **Try the demo**, or open `/demo` once the update is published.
No login, test account, personal account connection or purchase is required.

## What to try
- Choose **Check this screenshot** for a real 2014 Altima listing or a 2011 BMW listing whose seller states the engine is seized. Private details have been removed. These are saved, unverified listings, not recommendations or confirmed available cars.
- Choose one of three explicitly fictional listings: an everyday Corolla, an older Altima with admitted mechanical problems, or a Civic with payment-pressure scam signs.
- The real checker reads the listing and generates a report with estimated costs, model-year records and questions for the seller.
- Ask Scout: “What should I ask before meeting the seller?”
- Open Viewing mode and answer the checks. Try seller practice from the report.
- Select Español to try the experience in Spanish.
- Alternatively choose **Check a real listing** and paste listing text or upload a screenshot with seller contact details removed. Direct Facebook link reading is not supported.

## Limitations and privacy
- Text scenarios are fictional; screenshot examples are real saved listings with private details removed. Both generate fresh reports using live services, not simulated responses. A separate example report is illustrative and does not run a new check.
- Records apply to a model and model year. VIN decoding does not provide accident, ownership, title history or per-VIN recall repair status.
- Costs are budgets, not quotes or an all-inclusive ownership total. The report identifies inclusions and exclusions.
- External services and AI can be unavailable, slow or imperfect. Missing information is labeled rather than invented; users can edit extracted details.
- Normal usage protections remain: 10 car checks and 40 Scout messages per visitor per hour. Demo checks use the same allowances.
- Demo examples exclude seller identity and real buyer information. Original screenshots remain in English; Scout uses the selected language. Generated reports have private-by-possession links; anyone with a report link can read that report. Do not use real personal details for judging.
- Chat and viewing answers stay in the testing browser and are not included in shared report links.
- No email delivery, payment processing or full vehicle-history service is offered.
