## Product spec review

Read the full `product-spec.md` for product understanding only. Used Car Scout helps first-time US used-car buyers understand risks and estimated ownership costs using NHTSA and fueleconomy.gov data. The core journey is listing text/screenshot → confirm vehicle → report, with Scout questions and an at-car checklist. It prioritizes real sources, clearly labeled estimates, no accounts, safety, and minimal personal data collection.

The spec contains older Figma-derived layout instructions and references to prepared screens. Those are not approved design references under the user's current hackathon constraints and must not be used to recreate their screens. No app features or Cloud services were built or enabled by this preparation task.
